# Professional License Tracker

## Problem Statement

Professionals with state licenses face renewal complexity:
- Requirements vary significantly by state and profession type
- Continuing education credit tracking across multiple providers
- Manual deadline management leads to lapses (career-ending in some professions)
- Employers struggle to verify staff license status across multiple states
- Professional organizations need compliance reporting for members

## Solution

Automated professional license monitoring and renewal assistance:

**Individual Features**:
- License status monitoring across all states
- Continuing education credit tracking and planning
- Automated renewal reminders with state-specific requirements
- Document organization and submission assistance
- Career compliance history and reporting

**Employer Features**:
- Staff license monitoring dashboard
- Bulk renewal management and payment
- Compliance reporting for audits
- Integration with HR systems
